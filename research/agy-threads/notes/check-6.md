Wayback Machine captures of the official iRO Community Bug Board from June 2004 (bugcontent.asp) preserve verbatim in-game text and mechanics, including pet speech strings (Bug #4201: 'Wahh, too much food!', 'For the first time in my life, I'm full. You complete me.'), player confirmation that Job 50 Mages expected to skip the first Wizard quest stage (Bug #4218), and an official script flaw allowing players to skip the Assassin written test by canceling dialogue (Bug #4222).

## Verdict
Confirmed. Wayback Machine snapshots of Gravity Interactive's official iRO Bug Report Board (`bugcontent.asp` / `bugContent.asp`) from June 2004 preserve authentic contemporaneous player reports containing verbatim in-game dialogue, official script bugs, and expected quest mechanics.

- Pet speech strings preserved verbatim (Bug #4201) — "Hi, my pet Bongun recently began speaking, but his speech set seems to be mixed up with that of a Poring-- some of the things he says are: \" Wahh, too much food!\" \"I think I'm gonna throw up...\" \"For the first time in my life, I'm full. You complete me.\" \"Fattening me up for slaughter, eh? I'm watching you...\" \"Bananas again? With this many, I'll turn yellow and become a Drops?\" \"Yummy yummy in my tummy\" I've heard that a lot (if not all) that he says is identical to the Porings, so I was wondering if this is a bug that could be fixed sometime in the near future." — https://web.archive.org/web/20040623161452/http://iro.ragnarokonline.com/community/bugcontent.asp?mNum=2&seqNo=4201&page=2&serOption=&sertext= (published 2004-06-04; high)
- Job 50 Wizard quest mechanics expectation (Bug #4218) — "I am currently Mage job level 50 but the guildsman is still asking me for the items of the first test. I was under the impression at job level 50 we get to skip the first test. So why is it they are still asking me to take the first test?" — https://web.archive.org/web/20040608071858/http://iro.ragnarokonline.com/community/bugcontent.asp?mNum=2&seqNo=4218&page=1&serOption=&sertext= (published 2004-06-05; high)
- Official Assassin written test bypass flaw (Bug #4222) — "I recently turned sin, as in last night, and found something interesting. The hardest part of the test, the written part, is flawed. I didn't answer a question and was turned assassin. might wanna fix that :) not that i mind of course, was getting mad at getting 80 / 100 and getting failed." — https://web.archive.org/web/20040608124530/http://iro.ragnarokonline.com/community/bugContent.asp?mNum=2&seqNo=4222&page=1&serOption=&sertext= (published 2004-06-05; high)
