### Novice Quest Skills
1. **First Aid**
   - **Required Job Level:** 5
   - **Quest Items:** 3 Red Herbs, 3 Clovers, 1 Sterilized Bandage
   - **NPC Location:** Inside Prontera, Prontera Castle (Nurse Officer)
   - **Mechanics:** 3 SP into 5 HP. Applicable to all jobs.
2. **Play Dead**
   - **Required Job Level:** 7 Novice with First Aid
   - **Quest Items:** 1 Newbie Tag
   - **NPC Location:** Prontera Chivalry, Prontera Castle (Nurse Officer)
   - **Mechanics:** SP Required: 7. Allows character to play dead to drop monster aggro; cannot be used after changing jobs.

### Swordsman Quest Skills
1. **Berserk (Auto Berserk)**
   - **Required Job Level:** 30
   - **Quest Items:** 35 Powder of Butterfly, 10 Honey, 10 Horrendous Mouth, 10 Decayed Nails
   - **NPC Location:** Inside Prontera
   - **Mechanics:** Passive. When HP is less than 25%, automatically applies Level 10 Provoke (+32% ATK, -55% DEF). Cancels once HP is above 25%.
2. **Fatal Blow**
   - **Required Job Level:** 30
   - **Quest Items:** 30 Tentacles, 10 Fire Arrows, 10 Silver Arrows, 10 Banana Juice, 5 Royal Jelly
   - **NPC Location:** Prontera Chivalry
   - **Mechanics:** Passive. Enables Stun chance on Level 6-10 Bash:
     * Bash Lv 6: 6% chance
     * Bash Lv 7: 12% chance
     * Bash Lv 8: 18% chance
     * Bash Lv 9: 24% chance
     * Bash Lv 10: 30% chance
3. **HP Recovery while Moving**
   - **Required Job Level:** 35
   - **Quest Items:** 200 Empty Bottles, 1 Moth Wing, 1 Armor (any; the NPC returns the armor)
   - **NPC Location:** Inside Izlude
   - **Mechanics:** Passive. Recovers 25% of standard HP recovery rate while moving (unaffected by Increase HP Recovery).

### Magician Quest Skill
1. **Energy Coat**
   - **Required Job Level:** 35
   - **Quest Items:** 5 Shells, 1 Solid Shell, 3 Glass Beads, 1 1-Carat Diamond
   - **NPC Location:** Geffen Mage Guild
   - **Mechanics:** Active, 30 SP. Lasts 5 minutes or until SP is drained. Reduces physical damage taken based on remaining SP percentage:
     * 1–20% SP: 6% Damage Reduction, 1.0% SP Drain/Hit
     * 21–40% SP: 12% Damage Reduction, 1.5% SP Drain/Hit
     * 41–60% SP: 18% Damage Reduction, 2.0% SP Drain/Hit
     * 61–80% SP: 24% Damage Reduction, 2.5% SP Drain/Hit
     * 81–100% SP: 30% Damage Reduction, 3.0% SP Drain/Hit

## 3. Page 3: GameFAQs FAQ 18644 (Weapon Guide)
- **URL:** `https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/pc/561051-ragnarok-online/faqs/18644`
- **Title:** Ragnarok Online - Weapon Guide - PC - By RoboShop
- **Version / Date:** Release 2 | Fri 22 Nov 2002
- **Research Objective:** Period weapon refine fees, materials, safe limits, locations, and damage mechanics.
- **Findings & Verbatim Data:**

### Upgrade Shop Locations and NPCs
Upgrade shops are located in four towns:
1. **Alberta:** Merchant Guild (bottom-left corner building), north room
2. **Morroc:** Bottom-left corner building, basement
3. **Payon:** Armory (top-left corner building), west room
4. **Prontera:** Building down and right from the center statue (half a screen straight down from Armory)

Each upgrade shop contains three NPCs:
- NPC 1: Sells Phracon (200z) and Emveretarcon (1,000z). Purchased via NPC dialog interface (Merchant Discount does NOT apply).
- NPC 2: Pure ore refiner: converts 5 Rough Elunium -> 1 Elunium, 5 Rough Oridecon -> 1 Oridecon.
- NPC 3: Refiner blacksmith who performs the weapon upgrades.

### Upgrade Data by Weapon Level Table
| Weapon Level | Refine Fee | Required Ore | Safe Point | ATK Per +1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Weapon Level 1 | 50z | Phracon | +7 | +2 |
| Weapon Level 2 | 200z | Emveretarcon | +6 | +3 |
| Weapon Level 3 | 1000z? | Oridecon | +5 | +5 |
| Weapon Level 4 | 10000z? | Oridecon | +5 | +5 |

- **Maximum Refine:** +10.
- **Failure Risk:** 100% success up to the Safe Point. Beyond the safe point, each upgrade has an estimated ~50% failure rate. In this early period, failure reduced the weapon back to non-upgraded state (or broke it).
- **Damage Mechanics:** Refine ATK bonus is flat BASE damage that adds directly to STR bonus (`STR + [STR/10]^2`) and is NOT reduced by monster size penalties or hard defense.

## 4. Page 4: GameFAQs FAQ 30075 (Perma-Novice Guide)
- **URL:** `https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/pc/561051-ragnarok-online/faqs/30075`
- **Title:** Ragnarok Online - Perma-Novice Guide - PC - By Mad_Monarch_Gyl
- **Version / Date:** Version 2.6 | Updated: 05/16/2004
- **Research Objective:** Novice Training ground walkthrough and coordinates.
- **Finding:** The guide **does not** contain a walkthrough or coordinates for the instanced/tutorial Novice Training Ground (`new_zone` / `new_1-1` through `new_1-4`).
- **Details:** The author's use of the term "Novice Hunting Grounds" refers strictly to open-world field maps suited for level 1–20 novices:
  * `prt_fild08` ("Perhaps one of the best Novice Hunting Grounds there is... [1 South] from Prontera / [1 West] from Izlude")
  * `prt_fild06` ("Novice Hunting Ground... [1 East] from Prontera")
  * `moc_fild07` ("A very good Novice Hunting Ground... [1 North] from Morroc")
  * `gef_fild00` and `gef_fild07`
- The tutorial castle itself is never walked through or coordinated in this guide.
