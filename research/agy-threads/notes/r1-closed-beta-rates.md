# Thread #1: euRO Closed-Beta Rates

## Answer so far
- **Closed Beta (2003-12-20 to 2004-01-31)**: Operated with boosted experience and drop rates ("higher experience"). BHI-OE General Manager Imdat Solak ("PerrI") officially announced on 2003-12-20: *"As we are testing with higher experience, we will delete all characters and/or accounts at the end of the Closed Beta."* On 2004-01-27, German fan site staff member Astartus (*ragnarok.gamona.de*) confirmed: *"Die Closed Beta Accounts werden sie sicher nicht versuchen zu übernehmen, da die Zeit einfach zu kurz, und die EXP und Drop Rate zu hoch war."* However, **no specific numerical multiplier (e.g. 2x, 3x, 5x) was ever officially published or recorded**. The players themselves did not know the exact rates, creating forum topics on `forum.euro.bhi-oe.de` such as *"what's the exactly exp/Droprate atm?"* (Topic 67) and *"[ Poll ] Exp and Droprate : Higher or high enough :D?"* (Topic 80).
- **Open Beta (2004-02-04 to 2004-04-13)**: Standard official **1x Base EXP / 1x Job EXP / 1x Drop Rate**. Following the total wipe of Closed Beta on 2004-01-31, Open Beta ran at 1x rates. On 2004-04-08, Prontera Tourism Office officially announced that Open Beta characters would **not** be wiped, and carried over directly into commercial service (with items/zeny snapshotted on 2004-04-08 09:00 CET).
- **Commercial Service / P2P (2004-04-15 onwards)**: Standard official **1x Base EXP / 1x Job EXP / 1x Drop Rate** (official Gravity baseline).

## Findings

### 1. Closed Beta Rates & Wipe Policy
- **Higher Experience Confirmed**: BHI-OE announced higher experience on launch day as justification for the full character wipe at closed beta's end.
  - *"VERSION The version of the Closed Beta includes Comodo and Q-Pet. RULES In order to have a successful beta test, we need to set some rules. [...] As we are testing with higher experience, we will delete all characters and/or accounts at the end of the Closed Beta."* — `http://web.archive.org/web/20031223001708/http://euro.bhi-oe.de:80/` (published 2003-12-20 17:07 CET by PerrI / Imdat Solak; [READ]; high confidence).
- **Drop Rate Also Boosted**: German community staff acknowledged that both EXP and Drop rates were excessively high during Closed Beta.
  - *"Die Closed Beta Accounts werden sie sicher nicht versuchen zu übernehmen, da die Zeit einfach zu kurz, und die EXP und Drop Rate zu hoch war."* — `http://web.archive.org/web/20040213093633/http://ragnarok.gamona.de/forum/index.php?act=ST&f=31&t=9857` (published 2004-01-27 22:15 CET by Astartus; [READ]; high confidence).
- **Community Ignorance of Exact Rates (Negative Result)**: The exact multiplier was never documented by BHI-OE, leaving players asking and polling on `forum.euro.bhi-oe.de` (forum `f=3`):
  - Topic 67: *"what's the exactly exp/Droprate atm?"* — 12 replies, author Kuja, 401 views, last post 2003-12-25 22:44 CET by kon1 — `http://web.archive.org/web/20031227055051/http://forum.euro.bhi-oe.de:80/viewforum.php?f=3&amp` (published 2003-12-25; [READ]; high confidence).
  - Topic 80: *"[ Poll ] Exp and Droprate : Higher or high enough :D?"* — 11 replies, author hentaifreak03, 489 views, last post 2003-12-24 09:29 CET by hentaifreak03 — `http://web.archive.org/web/20031227055051/http://forum.euro.bhi-oe.de:80/viewforum.php?f=3&amp` (published 2003-12-24; [READ]; high confidence).
- **Extreme Leveling Pace in Closed Beta**:
  - Knight `-Barak-` reached Level 64/34 by 2003-12-24 (just 4 days after launch on 2003-12-20) — `http://web.archive.org/web/20031227055437/http://forum.euro.bhi-oe.de:80/viewtopic.php?p=2187&amp` ([READ]; high confidence).
  - Wizard `Alan` reached Level 73/42 by 2004-01-18 — `http://web.archive.org/web/20040213074848/http://ragnarok.gamona.de/forum/index.php?act=ST&f=31&t=9778` ([READ]; high confidence).
  - Priestess `Maria` reached Level 70/40 by 2004-01-28 — `http://web.archive.org/web/20040405185100/http://forum.euro.bhi-oe.de:80/viewtopic.php?t=2539` ([READ]; high confidence).

### 2. Transition & Wipe of Closed Beta
- **Closed Beta Shutdown**: Ended Saturday, January 31, 2004 at 13:00 CET with a complete character, item, and account wipe.
  - *"During the preparation, we will wipe (delete) all characters, items, and accounts. You will need to re-register starting on Monday, February 2nd, 2004."* — `http://web.archive.org/web/20040405184353/http://forum.euro.bhi-oe.de:80/viewtopic.php?t=2400` (published 2004-01-27 19:12 CET by PerrI; [READ]; high confidence).
- **Open Beta Launch Postponement**: Postponed from Feb 2 to Feb 4, 2004 at 10:00 CET.
  - *"Due to some internal problems, we have to postpone the launch of the Open Beta to FEBRUARY 4th, 2004, 10:00am CET."* — `http://web.archive.org/web/20040405185100/http://forum.euro.bhi-oe.de:80/viewtopic.php?t=2539` (published 2004-02-02 06:58 CET by PerrI; [READ]; high confidence).

### 3. Open Beta and Commercial Rates
- **Open Beta Carryover into Commercial (Proving 1x Rates)**: Open Beta characters were retained into Commercial Service to preserve game economy; rates were 1x.
  - *"Decision regarding Wipe (Reset) April, 8th 2004: Prontera Tourism Office announces today that the Open Beta characters will not be deleted. To maintain a working game economy on the 15th of April all characters will have the items and Zeny inventory of today (8th of April) 9 am CET. This means that you still can level your characters and open Accounts but all items and all Zeny which your character earns or takes into posession from this time up until 13th of April will be deleted... Server will go offline at 10am CET on April 13th and will go online again at 10am CET April 15th."* — `http://web.archive.org/web/20040423025155/http://www.euro-ro.net:80/news/eu/00054/` (published 2004-04-08; [READ]; high confidence).
  - German release text: *"Das Prontera Tourism Office gibt heute bekannt, dass die Open Beta Charaktere bestehen bleiben. Um weiterhin eine funktionierende Wirtschaft sicherzustellen, wird am 15.04.2004 der Item und Zeny Stand von heute (08.04.2004) 9 Uhr CET genommen..."* — `http://web.archive.org/web/20040510025242/http://www.euro-ro.net:80/community/forum/viewtopic.php?t=5283&amp` (published 2004-04-08 11:11 CET; [READ]; high confidence).
- **Commercial Launch (2004-04-15)**: Standard official 1x rates.
  - *"Commercial Start April, 15th 2004: Prontera Tourism Office announces today that the P2P (Pay to Play) business of the servers started and the servers are online again."* — `http://web.archive.org/web/20040423025128/http://www.euro-ro.net:80/news/eu/00109/` (published 2004-04-15; [READ]; high confidence).

## Contradictions and Open Points
- **Exact Numeric Multiplier for Closed Beta**:
  - Neither BHI-OE nor Gravity published the numerical multiplier for Base EXP, Job EXP, or Drop Rate during the closed beta.
  - Contemporary forum threads (`t=67` and `t=80`) confirm that the community itself was actively trying to determine the exact multiplier through guesswork, observation, and player polls, proving that the operator kept the multiplier internal.
  - The thread bodies of `t=67` and `t=80` were not archived by the Wayback Machine crawlers in Dec 2003 (only the forum index `viewforum.php?f=3&amp` was archived on 2003-12-27).
  - Based on player progression speeds (characters reaching 64-73 in days) and period private/test server setups, community speculation was typically between 2x and 5x, but there is no verified primary source confirming an exact integer.
