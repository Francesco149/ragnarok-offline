# Fact-Check: euRO Closed Beta Rates, Multipliers, and Open Beta Transition

## Verdict
**Partly confirmed** (substantially accurate on all dates, operational phases, and wipe policies, with one scope correction regarding official attribution of drop rates):
- **Dates**: Closed Beta ran from **December 20, 2003** (17:07 CET) to **January 31, 2004** (13:00 CET). Open Beta launched on **February 4, 2004** (10:00 CET, postponed from February 2) and transitioned into commercial service on **April 15, 2004**.
- **Closed Beta Boosted Rates**: BHI-OE officially announced higher *experience* on launch day ("As we are testing with higher experience, we will delete all characters and/or accounts at the end of the Closed Beta"). However, BHI-OE never explicitly mentioned *drop rates* in official announcements; boosted drop rates were recognized and discussed as part of community consensus and fansite staff statements (*ragnarok.gamona.de*).
- **Multiplier Secrecy & Speculation**: Neither BHI-OE nor Gravity ever published the exact integer multiplier for rates. Forum threads on `forum.euro.bhi-oe.de` (Topic 67: *"what's the exactly exp/Droprate atm?"*; Topic 80: *"[ Poll ] Exp and Droprate : Higher or high enough :D?"*) and Gamona demonstrate that players were left to guess and speculate (e.g., 3x or 2x–5x), aided by rapid leveling observations (e.g., Level 64 in 4 days).
- **Open Beta Reversion to 1x**: Open Beta operated at standard official Gravity 1x rates following a complete account/character wipe on January 31, 2004.
- **Carryover to Commercial Service**: On April 8, 2004, the Prontera Tourism Office officially confirmed that Open Beta characters would **not** be wiped upon commercial start (April 15, 2004), though item and Zeny inventories were snapshotted to April 8, 09:00 CET to safeguard the economy.

### Corrected Wording
"euRO Closed Beta (Dec 20, 2003 – Jan 31, 2004) operated with boosted experience (officially stated by BHI-OE) and boosted drop rates (acknowledged by community staff and player consensus), but the operator never published an exact integer multiplier (leaving players to speculate 2x–5x on forums); following a full Closed Beta wipe, Open Beta launched on Feb 4, 2004 at standard 1x rates, and characters carried over into commercial service on April 15, 2004 without a character wipe (with inventory snapshotted to April 8 to protect the economy)."

## Findings

- **Closed Beta Launch & Official Higher Experience**: BHI-OE announced the launch of Closed Beta on December 20, 2003, stating higher experience as the reason for an eventual full wipe, without stating an exact multiplier or mentioning drop rates.
  - *"VERSION The version of the Closed Beta includes Comodo and Q-Pet. RULES In order to have a successful beta test, we need to set some rules. [...] As we are testing with higher experience, we will delete all characters and/or accounts at the end of the Closed Beta."* — `http://web.archive.org/web/20031223001708/http://euro.bhi-oe.de:80/` (published 2003-12-20 17:07 CET by PerrI / Imdat Solak; high confidence).
  - *"closed beta is started"* — `http://web.archive.org/web/20031223001708/http://euro.bhi-oe.de:80/` (published 2003-12-20; high confidence).

- **Community Acknowledgment of Boosted EXP and Drop Rates**: Staff and players on German portal *Ragnarok no Seishi* (*ragnarok.gamona.de*) confirmed that both EXP and Drop rates were heavily boosted during Closed Beta.
  - *"Die Closed Beta Accounts werden sie sicher nicht versuchen zu übernehmen, da die Zeit einfach zu kurz, und die EXP und Drop Rate zu hoch war."* — `http://web.archive.org/web/20040213093633/http://ragnarok.gamona.de/forum/index.php?act=ST&f=31&t=9857` (published 2004-01-27 21:43 CET by Astartus; high confidence).
  - *"Nein Exp und Droprate bleibt nicht wie sie in der closed war (nämlich 3fach). Sie wird ganz normal einfach sein ..."* — `http://web.archive.org/web/20040213093633/http://ragnarok.gamona.de/forum/index.php?act=ST&f=31&t=9857` (published 2004-01-27 21:24 CET by Pandaroc; high confidence).

- **No Official Multiplier & Player Speculation on Forums**: BHI-OE maintained rate secrecy; players created forum threads seeking the exact multiplier and polling opinions on the rates.
  - Topic 67: *"what's the exactly exp/Droprate atm?"* — 12 replies, author Kuja, 401 views — `http://web.archive.org/web/20031227055051/http://forum.euro.bhi-oe.de:80/viewforum.php?f=3&amp` (published 2003-12-25; high confidence).
  - Topic 80: *"[ Poll ] Exp and Droprate : Higher or high enough :D?"* — 11 replies, author hentaifreak03, 489 views — `http://web.archive.org/web/20031227055051/http://forum.euro.bhi-oe.de:80/viewforum.php?f=3&amp` (published 2003-12-24; high confidence).
  - Level pacing during Closed Beta: Knight `-Barak-` reached Lv 64/34 by Dec 24, 2003 (`http://web.archive.org/web/20031227055437/http://forum.euro.bhi-oe.de:80/viewtopic.php?p=2187&amp`); Wizard `Alan` reached Lv 73/42 by Jan 18, 2004 (`http://web.archive.org/web/20040213074848/http://ragnarok.gamona.de/forum/index.php?act=ST&f=31&t=9778`).

- **Closed Beta Shutdown and Complete Wipe**: Closed Beta servers shut down on Saturday, January 31, 2004 at 13:00 CET, wiping all characters, items, and accounts.
  - *"In order to prepare the launch of Open Beta on February 2nd, 2004, we will shutdown our servers from: Saturday, January 31st, 2004, 13:00 CET until Monday, February 2nd, 2004, 10:00 CET [...] During the preparation, we will wipe (delete) all characters, items, and accounts. You will need to re-register starting on Monday, February 2nd, 2004."* — `http://web.archive.org/web/20040405184353/http://forum.euro.bhi-oe.de:80/viewtopic.php?t=2400` (published 2004-01-27 19:12 CET by PerrI / Imdat Solak; high confidence).

- **Open Beta Postponement to February 4, 2004**: Open Beta did not launch on February 2 as originally planned, but was postponed by two days to February 4, 2004.
  - *"Due to some internal problems, we have to postpone the launch of the Open Beta to FEBRUARY 4th, 2004, 10:00am CET. We apologize for the inconvenience."* — `http://web.archive.org/web/20040405185100/http://forum.euro.bhi-oe.de:80/viewtopic.php?t=2539` (published 2004-02-02 06:58 CET by PerrI; high confidence).

- **Open Beta Characters Carried Over Into Commercial Service Without Wipe**: On April 8, 2004, BHI-OE announced that Open Beta characters would be retained for commercial launch on April 15, 2004, with items and Zeny frozen to April 8 to prevent economic destabilization.
  - *"Prontera Tourism Office announces today that the Open Beta characters will not be deleted. To maintain a working game economy on the 15th of April all characters will have the items and Zeny inventory of today (8th of April) 9 am CET. This means that you still can level your characters and open Accounts but all items and all Zeny which your character earns or takes into posession from this time up until 13th of April will be deleted. Of course your old items and Zeny which you had before 8th of April will not be deleted. Guild castles will be handled the same way. [...] The Server will be go offline at 10am CET on April 13th and will go online again at 10am CET April 15th. This decision means that all Open Beta account users will have to pay from April 15th."* — `http://web.archive.org/web/20040423025155/http://www.euro-ro.net:80/news/eu/00054/` (published 2004-04-08; high confidence).
  - German announcement: *"Das Prontera Tourism Office gibt heute bekannt, dass die Open Beta Charaktere bestehen bleiben. Um weiterhin eine funktionierende Wirtschaft sicherzustellen, wird am 15.04.2004 der Item und Zeny Stand von heute (08.04.2004) 9 Uhr CET genommen..."* — `http://web.archive.org/web/20040510025242/http://www.euro-ro.net:80/community/forum/viewtopic.php?t=5283&amp` (published 2004-04-08 11:11 CET; high confidence).

- **Commercial Service Launch on April 15, 2004**: Commercial P2P service started on April 15, 2004, operating at standard 1x rates.
  - *"Commercial Start April, 15th 2004: Prontera Tourism Office announces today that the P2P (Pay to Play) business of the servers started and the servers are online again. We like to thank again all of the over 50.000 registered users for their help during the Open Beta..."* — `http://web.archive.org/web/20040423025128/http://www.euro-ro.net:80/news/eu/00109/` (published 2004-04-15; high confidence).

## Contradictions and Open Points

1. **Official Attribution of Drop Rates**:
   - The claim states that BHI-OE officially stated boosted experience *and* drop rates.
   - Primary official records (`http://euro.bhi-oe.de:80/`, 2003-12-20) prove BHI-OE explicitly stated: *"As we are testing with higher experience, we will delete all characters and/or accounts at the end of the Closed Beta."* BHI-OE did not mention drop rates in official posts.
   - However, drop rates were consistently acknowledged as boosted by fansite staff (Astartus on Gamona: *"die EXP und Drop Rate zu hoch war"*) and player discussions (Topic 67 & Topic 80).
2. **Economic Rollback Condition on Character Carryover**:
   - Characters themselves were not wiped between Open Beta and Commercial Service.
   - However, to prevent runaway inflation, items and Zeny were frozen to an April 8, 2004 09:00 CET snapshot (any items or Zeny earned between April 8 and April 13 were deleted upon commercial start).
