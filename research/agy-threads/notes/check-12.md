## Verdict
**Confirmed.** The Izlude time-attack Arena (Time Force Battle Arena) did not exist anywhere in Dec 2003 – Jan 2004. On kRO, the north Izlude arena entrance was first referenced on 2004-06-01 (when a statue was placed in front of it commemorating the Novice Siege), detailed in a dev notice on 2004-09-08, opened on Sakray on 2004-09-14, and officially launched on kRO main servers on 2004-10-12 ("오랜시간의 재공사끝에 이즈루드 마을에 아레나가 추가되었습니다."). The arena scripts present in eAthena 817 (`other/arena.txt`, imported as `scripts/import/npc/other/arena.lua`) were fan-made test scripts set in Prontera (`prontera 160, 185`) warping players to `prt_are_in` and `force_map1` (`force_1-1`) to kill Porings, Roda Frogs, and PecoPecos within 30–100 seconds. Because they are custom emulator creations and official Izlude Arena was not released until late 2004, they must be disabled for late-2003 accuracy.
