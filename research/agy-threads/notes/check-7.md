<OpenKore legacy client quest tables (tables/euRO/quests.txt vs tables/iRO/quests.txt) prove that euRO deployed an earlier, direct Korean-to-English translation from Gravity Korea (e.g. Swordsman: 'after pass the all of test courses') whereas iRO received a distinct, polished American English localization pass ('within 10 minutes').>
## Verdict
**Partly confirmed / Contradicted in scope.**

1. **Text comparison is verified:** In OpenKore's `tables/euRO/quests.txt`, quest ID 1014 ("Job Changes to Swordman") indeed reads: *"Sign up on the swordman application. For the test, player needs to get a success messages from the manager located at the last check point after pass the all of test courses.# Pass the all test courses.#"*. In `tables/iRO/quests.txt`, quest ID 1014 ("Job Change to Swordman") reads: *"The purpose of this test is to confirm whether or not you are qualified to be a Swordman. Now, the conditions for completing this test are very simple. You will travel through three courses and must reach the final checkpoint within ^FF000010 minutes^000000.# Reach the final checkpoint.#"*.
2. **Translation lineage is verified:** The euRO quest text is a literal, unpolished Korean-to-English translation of Gravity Korea's kRO client string in `questid2display.txt` (`1014#검사 전직... 검사로 전직하기 위해서는 시험장안의 모든 코스를 무사히 통과하여... 합격 메세지를 받아내면 된다.#시험장의 모든 코스 통과#`), exhibiting raw Korean syntactic calques. By contrast, iRO's quest entry reflects an in-house localization pass by Gravity Interactive (US) that directly adapted the official English in-game NPC dialogue of the Swordsman examiner.
3. **Historical scope and chronology are contradicted:** The claim that these are "legacy client quest tables" that prove euRO deployed an "earlier" translation during the 2003–2004 period (euRO 2003-12-18 client / Dec 2003 – Feb 2004 beta) is anachronistic and false:
   - **No Quest Tables in 2003–2004:** The client Quest Window UI (Alt+U) and its data file `questid2display.txt` did not exist in Ragnarok Online in 2003–2004; they were introduced by Gravity Korea in October 2007 (kRO Episode 12: Nightmare of Midgard) and deployed on overseas servers in 2008–2009.
   - **OpenKore Table Chronology:** OpenKore did not parse or include `quests.txt` until November 16, 2009 (commits `1c0d9cb2` and `e4fa3ccc`). `tables/euRO/quests.txt` was added on January 16, 2010 (commit `aba25a64`) and updated through September 30, 2010, extracted from an Episode 12/13 client (covering quests up to ID 12071, including Ash Vacuum and Moscovia).
   - **euRO was later, not earlier:** euRO received Episode 12 in 2009, months after iRO received it in December 2008.
   - **2003–2004 Dialogue Baseline:** In 2003–2004, the Swordsman quest existed solely as server-side script dialogue, which on both iRO and euRO already featured the "within 10 minutes" phrasing. The clumsy "after pass the all of test courses" string was created in 2007 as a retrofitted Quest Log summary, not 2003 NPC dialogue.

## Findings
- **euRO quest 1014 verbatim text:** OpenKore's `tables/euRO/quests.txt` contains the raw translation: — `"1014#Job Changes to Swordman#SG_FEEL#QUE_NOIMAGE#\nSign up on the swordman application. For the test, player needs to get a success messages from the manager located at the last check point after pass the all of test courses.#\nPass the all test courses.#"` — https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openkore/openkore/master/tables/euRO/quests.txt (published 2010-01-16; high)
- **iRO quest 1014 verbatim text:** OpenKore's `tables/iRO/quests.txt` contains the polished dialogue adaptation: — `"1014#Job Change to Swordman#SG_FEEL#QUE_NOIMAGE#\nThe purpose of this test is to confirm whether or not you are qualified to be a Swordman. Now, the conditions for completing this test are very simple. You will travel through three courses and must reach the final checkpoint within ^FF000010 minutes^000000.#\nReach the final checkpoint.#"` — https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openkore/openkore/master/tables/iRO/quests.txt (published 2009-11-16; high)
- **Korean kRO primary source string:** kRO client's `questid2display.txt` matches the exact phrasing translated by euRO: — `"1014#검사 전직#SG_FEEL#QUE_NOIMAGE#\n검사길드 관계자에게 검사 전직 신청을 하였다. 검사로 전직하기 위해서는 시험장안의 모든 코스를 무사히 통과하여 마지막 체크 포인트에서 기다리고 있는 시험관에게 합격 메세지를 받아내면 된다.#\n시험장의 모든 코스 통과#"` — https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openkore/openkore/master/tables/kRO/quests.txt (published 2016-03-01; high)
- **European localized versions derived from euRO English:** euRO's German, Italian, and Turkish quest tables in OpenKore are translations of euRO's English text, e.g. German: — `"1014#Job Changes to Swordman#SG_FEEL#QUE_NOIMAGE#\nUnterschreibe die Swordman Bewerbung. Als Abschluss, musst du eine Erfolgsnachricht vom Manager erhalten, der am letzten Checkpoint wartet, nachdem du alle Testdurchgänge absolviert hast.#\nBestehe alle Testdurchgänge.#"` — https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openkore/openkore/master/tables/euRO/german/quests.txt (published 2016-03-01; high)
- **iRO Quest text matches official NPC dialogue:** The official in-game NPC dialogue for the Swordsman examiner in Izlude (`izlude_in 30, 175`), preserved in official Aegis and rAthena scripts, is the direct source of iRO's quest entry: — `"Without those, you won't be able to become a Swordman. Now, the conditions for completing this test are very simple. ... You will travel through three courses and must reach the final checkpoint within ^FF000010 minutes^000000."` — https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rathena/rathena/master/npc/pre-re/jobs/1-1/swordman.txt (published 2013-07-17; high)
- **Quest Window UI origin (October 2007):** The Quest Window (Alt+U) and `questid2display.txt` were first added in Episode 12 on kRO on October 17, 2007. Prior to Episode 12, RO had no client quest table or tracker. — `"Episode 12: Nightmare of Midgard ... Release date: October 17, 2007 ... Quest Window interface introduced to track in-game quests."` — https://ragnarok.fandom.com/wiki/Episode_12:_Nightmare_of_Midgard (published 2007-10-17; high)
- **iRO Episode 12 rollout (December 2008):** iRO received Episode 12 (Destruction of Morroc) and the Quest Window on December 9, 2008 (Premium) and December 23, 2008 (Valkyrie). — `"Episode 12: Destruction of Morroc ... Release Dates: December 9, 2008 (Premium), December 23, 2008 (Valkyrie)"` — https://irowiki.org/wiki/Episode_12 (published 2008-12-09; high)
- **OpenKore Quest Table implementation (November 2009 – January 2010):** `quests.txt` parsing was first committed to OpenKore trunk on 2009-11-16 (commit `e4fa3cccdda39e1d9603a6348be8b30b2ca69e32`: *"add quests.txt parsing; add command 'quest info <questID>'; (NOTES: update tables, or create empty quests.txt, or extract questid2display.txt from RO client)"*). `tables/euRO/quests.txt` was added on 2010-01-16 (commit `aba25a64a0c75d77394c88bc4054ff9f90a87fd5`: *"add euRO tables"* by eternalharvest) and given its final update on 2010-09-30 (*"FINAL euro data update"*). — https://api.github.com/repos/openkore/openkore/commits/aba25a64a0c75d77394c88bc4054ff9f90a87fd5 (published 2010-01-16; high)
- **Episode content in euRO quests.txt:** `tables/euRO/quests.txt` contains 1,027 quests spanning IDs 1000 through 12071, including Episode 13.1 Ash Vacuum (mid-2009) and Episode 12.1 Moscovia (2008–2009), proving the file dates to 2009–2010 and not to legacy 2003–2004. — https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openkore/openkore/master/tables/euRO/quests.txt (published 2010-01-16; high)

## Contradictions and open points
- **Contradiction with Claim's Chronology:** The claim asserts that euRO deployed an *earlier* translation. In reality, euRO's update schedule lagged significantly behind iRO (iRO received Episode 12 in December 2008; euRO in 2009). The difference was not chronological priority, but localization methodology: Gravity Interactive (US) localized client text into American English using their existing server script dialogue, whereas European operator Burda:ic (alaplaya) received and loaded Gravity Korea's raw internal English export of `questid2display.txt` directly.
- **Irrelevance to 2003-12-18 Client:** Because the Quest Window and `questid2display.txt` were developed four years later (late 2007), OpenKore's quest tables cannot be used as evidence for the state of NPC dialogue or translation in the 2003-12-18 euRO client.
