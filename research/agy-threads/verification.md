# Fact-Check Verification Log

- **Date:** 2026-11-09
- **Brief:** Fact-checking 12 key claims for `ragnarok-offline` (euRO 2003-12-18 client, closed/open beta Dec 2003 – Feb 2004).

| Claim # | Topic | Verdict | Summary & Key Quotations |
|---|---|---|---|
| Check 1 | 2-1 Job Changes before New Comodo | **PARTLY CONFIRMED** | Confirmed that during the early euRO closed beta (Dec 20, 2003 – Jan 8/18, 2004), 2-1 second job advancements required zero quests or tests, granting instant advancement at Job Level 40; 2-1 quests were added with the New Comodo update (Jan 8/18, 2004). Nuance: Closed beta ended Jan 31 (not Jan 8), and the "King Tristram III emergency decree" dialogue was fan-written placeholder dialogue by Athena emulator authors (RoVeRT/Darkchild) rather than official Gravity Aegis text. |
| Check 2 | Armor Refine DEF Math | **PARTLY CONFIRMED** | Confirmed that armor refine does not grant +1 or +5 hard DEF; Gravity's 2003-02-04 kRO rebalance deliberately made refine DEF less efficient than base equipment DEF and lowered the safe refine cap from +5 to +4. In pre-renewal emulators and community testing, this is accurately represented as ~0.7 DEF per level (`refine_db.txt` 70, `sd->def += (refinedef + 50) / 100`). Nuance: Gravity never published an explicit "0.7" coefficient in patch notes, and the C formula is an emulator implementation of official behavior. |
| Check 3 | Phracon Price (200z vs 2000z Typo) | **CONFIRMED** | Phracon was 200z and Emveretarcon was 1,000z across all client databases, emulator shops, and period guides. The 2000z price on iRO's official website was an editorial typo that conflated the armor refine fee (2,000z) with the Phracon price. Mathematically proven by period guides: getting a Lv 1 weapon to safe +7 cost exactly 1,750z (7 x (200z + 50z fee)). |
| Check 4 | Start Map Aliasing (resnametable.txt) | **CONFIRMED** | euRO 2003-12-18 client internal table `data/resnametable.txt` inside `data.grf` explicitly maps `new_1-1.gat` through `new_5-1.gat` (along with .gnd, .rsw, and minimap .bmp files) to `new_zone01.gat`, proving the official Aegis server sent `new_1-1.gat, 53, 111` while the client transparently rendered `new_zone01.gat`. |
| Check 5 | Video Footage of Bridgehead Start | **PARTLY CONFIRMED** | Video GJO1B9ogKXY (frames 0:39–1:14) and 2003 period guides (IGN May 2003: "You'll start in the Novice training ground. Cross the bridge to your right and you will come upon a castle") visually and textually confirm that new Novices spawned on the western grassy platform and crossed the bridge east. Nuance: Video GJO1B9ogKXY was recorded on a private server (EosRO) running an Athena derivative, so its exact coordinate 53,111 is emulator-derived rather than independent official video proof. |
| Check 6 | iRO Bug Board Preservation | **CONFIRMED** | June 2004 captures of iRO Community Bug Board (bugcontent.asp) preserve verbatim in-game pet speech strings (Bug #4201: "Wahh, too much food!", "For the first time in my life, I'm full. You complete me."), confirmation that Job 50 Mages expected to skip Stage 1 item collection on the Wizard quest (Bug #4218), and the Assassin written test cancel bypass (Bug #4222). |
| Check 7 | Regional Translation Pipelines (euRO vs iRO) | **PARTLY CONFIRMED** | Confirmed that euRO received Gravity Korea's raw literal translation (Swordsman: "after pass the all of test courses") while iRO received Gravity Interactive's polished American English localization ("within 10 minutes"). Nuance: Client quest window tables (quests.txt) date from Episode 12+ (~2008–2010), so while they prove the existence of two distinct localization branches, quests.txt itself was not in the 2003 client. |
| Check 8 | Patcher Christmas Strings & Lutie Permanence | **PARTLY CONFIRMED** | Disassembly of `Ragnarok.exe` (built 2003-12-12) confirms hardcoded strings `2003-10-31gdata_xmas.gpf`, `notice_xmas.txt`, and the `strncmp(edi, "xmas", 4)` check at offset `0x51c4`. Correction: Rather than downloading the GPF, the routine checked if `2003-10-31gdata_xmas.gpf` existed locally; if missing, it displayed `notice_xmas.txt` and halted launch (acting as a version gate from Gravity's template). Lutie itself was already permanently integrated in `data.grf` with 320 assets since Episode 2 (Dec 2002). |
| Check 9 | WoE Chest Scaling & Guild Dungeon MVPs | **PARTLY CONFIRMED** | Daily Master Room chests scale by `floor(Economy / 5) + 4` (4–24 chests) at 00:01/00:00, dropping 20 God Item parts (0.10% Aegis / 0.80% eAthena). Guild dungeons (gld_dun01..04) held Eddga, Doppelganger, Maya, and Dark Lord on 8-hour respawn timers. Nuance: The four MVPs also spawned in public open-world maps/dungeons (not exclusive to guild dungeons), but guild dungeons offered private, realm-gated encounters. |
| Check 10 | Pre-2004-02-24 Kafra Fares | **CONFIRMED** | Prior to the 2004-02-24 kRO overhaul, Kafra teleport fares were distance-based: Prontera to Geffen was 1400z (817's 1800z deduction in code was a copy-paste bug from Morroc; jAthena shows 1400z menu and deduction), Alberta to Prontera was 2100z (817's first NPC menu showing 1800z was a typo), and direct warp from Prontera to Alberta did not exist. |
| Check 11 | Closed Beta Boosted Rates & 1x Launch | **PARTLY CONFIRMED** | BHI-OE officially stated "testing with higher experience" and Gamona staff/community consensus confirmed excessively high drop rates; no integer multiplier was ever published. Following a full wipe on Jan 31, Open Beta ran at stock 1x rates, and characters carried over to P2P on April 15 without a character wipe (with items/zeny rolled back to April 8). |
| Check 12 | Izlude Arena Timeline | **CONFIRMED** | Izlude Arena did not exist in Dec 2003 on euRO/iRO. First teased on kRO on 2004-06-01 (statue teaser), tested on Sakray on 2004-09-14, and released on kRO main servers on 2004-10-12 (ro.gnjoy.com seq=1785: "오랜시간의 재공사끝에 이즈루드 마을에 아레나가 추가되었습니다."). 817's arena scripts were Prontera test scripts. |
