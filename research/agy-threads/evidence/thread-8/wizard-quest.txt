Evidence for Thread #8: Wizard and 2-1 Job Quests

Source 1: eathena-v0.5.2 (2004-05-07) npc/jobs/wizard.txt
Translated by yoshiki, converted by RoVeRT
Passage:
Stage 1: Application & Item Collection
Worker Cartlin Medichi (gef_tower 111, 37):
Requires either:
- Group 1: Red Blood, Crystal Blue, Wind of Verdure, Green Live (10 of each)
- Group 2: Red Gemstone, Blue Gemstone, Yellow Gemstone (10 of each)
(Waived if Job Level 50).

Stage 2: Written Examination
Examiner Raul Expagarus (gef_tower 102, 24):
10 questions on elemental theory and monster properties. Passing score is 90%.

Stage 3: Practical Combat Trial
Gloomy Wizard (gef_tower 116, 110):
Warps into job_wiz (Water Room -> Earth Room -> Fire Room).
Player must eliminate elemental monsters within 3 minutes per room.
Fallback: If the player fails 4 times, they can bring a Worn Out Scroll to pass.

Comparison with other 2-1 jobs in eathena-v0.5.2:
Knight, Blacksmith, Assassin, and Hunter scripts remained simple Job Level 40 stubs waiving tests.
Priest had a custom Holy Grail script.
Wizard had the complete 1,563-line authentic test script.
