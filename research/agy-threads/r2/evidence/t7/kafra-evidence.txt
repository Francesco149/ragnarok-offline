4. euRO Timeline for Kafra Overhaul
- Official Updateplan (2004-04-01): `https://web.archive.org/web/20040406030155/http://www.euro-ro.net:80/news/eu/00010/`
- euRO ran the December 2003 Comodo client through Closed Beta (Dec 2003 - Jan 2004), Open Beta (Feb 2004 - Apr 2004), and commercial launch (April 15, 2004).
- The Juno update (which brought the Episode 8 engine and new Kafra tables to Western servers) was scheduled for June 2004. euRO maintained the authentic pre-2004-02-24 fare table until the Juno update.
