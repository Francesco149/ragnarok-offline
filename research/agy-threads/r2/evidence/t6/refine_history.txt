2. ANALYSIS OF DISCREPANCIES (GRADE X SETTLED):
- Phracon 2000z:
  Confirmed as a clerical typo on the iRO website. In the 2004-06-05 capture, Phracon was written as 2,000z, while Emveretarcon was 1,000z (making basic Phracon twice as expensive as Emveretarcon!). In the August 11, 2004 capture, webmaster corrected Phracon back to 200z. In the client binary and NPC scripts, Phracon is 200z.
- Level 3 vs Level 4 Weapon values:
  The June 2004 page listed Level 4 weapons with the exact same values as Level 3 (5,000z fee, +5 ATK bonus, +5 safe limit). This was stale pre-Comodo placeholder copy. The official kRO patch of 2003-02-04 updated Level 4 weapons to +7 ATK bonus, 20,000z fee, and +4 safe limit. The iRO website updated to reflect this in late 2004.
- Armor Defense (+1 adds 1 defense):
  In the pre-renewal client UI and item description, refining armor displayed "+1" DEF per refine level (e.g., +4 Chain Mail gave 8 + 4 DEF). However, server-side damage calculation applied refine defense as a separate fractional or direct reduction. Gravity's web page summarized this in simple terms as "+1 adds 1 defense".
