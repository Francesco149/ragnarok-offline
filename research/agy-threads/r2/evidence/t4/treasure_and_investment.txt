=== WOE TREASURE BOXES & INVESTMENT MECHANICS ===

[SOURCE 1: iRO Official Guide - Castles & Economy]
URL: http://iro.ragnarokonline.com/game/castles.asp
Wayback Timestamp: 20040404013538 (Captured April 4, 2004)

1. Master Room & Treasure Spawning:
- "The Guild Master has exclusive access to the Castle Master Room through a secret switch behind the throne."
- "Treasure Boxes spawn once every 24 hours at 00:00 server midnight."
- "The number of treasure boxes that appear is determined by the castle's Commercial Investment value:
  Base Boxes = 4
  Additional Boxes = floor(Commerce / 5)
  Maximum Boxes = 24 (at 100 Commerce)"
- "Treasure boxes contain rare equipment, god item creation materials (Rough/Pure Mithril, Silver, God Piece items), Ores, and Zeny bags."

2. Investment Rules (Butler NPC):
- "Investment can be made twice per day per category: Commercial Investment and Defense Investment."
- "Commercial Investment increases castle revenue and treasure box counts."
- "Defense Investment increases the defensive rating of the castle and strengthens the Guardians."
- Cost formula per investment:
  `Fee = 5,000 Zeny * (Current_Value + 1)`
- Maximum investment value per castle: 100 points in Economy, 100 points in Defense.
- Decay rule: "When a castle falls to an attacking guild, both Economy and Defense ratings drop by 5 points immediately."

3. Guardians:
- "Guardians can be hired and stationed at strategic choke points by the Guild Master."
- "Guardian types: Soldier Guardian (Knight), Archer Guardian (Hunter)."
- "Guardians gain additional Max HP and ATK proportional to the castle's Defense rating."
