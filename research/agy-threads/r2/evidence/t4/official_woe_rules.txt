=== OFFICIAL WAR OF EMPERIUM RULES & MECHANICS (PERIOD 2003-2004) ===

Combat & Map Restrictions inside Agit:
1. "Knock-back skills will not inflict knock-back effects on enemies."
2. "Endure effect will be canceled."
3. "Skills that reveal hidden enemies will not work on Stalkers or Thieves in Hiding or Cloaking unless they are inside the Emperium room."
4. "Damage done by normal attacks is reduced to 80%."
5. "Damage done by magic and physical skills is reduced to 60%."
6. "Traps and area-of-effect skills will affect both allies and enemies unless cast by a party member."
7. "When a character dies in the Agit, they do not lose Experience points, but are automatically returned to their save point."
8. "Teleportation and Fly Wings cannot be used inside the Agit castle."

Emperium Rules:
1. "The Emperium is a Holy property plant-type monster with 100,000 HP."
2. "Only characters in a Guild that has acquired the 'Official Guild Approval' skill can damage the Emperium."
3. "All damage inflicted on the Emperium is reduced to 1 per hit, except for critical hits and certain skills."
4. "When the Emperium is broken, all characters not belonging to the conquering guild are instantly expelled from the castle to the castle entrance."

Grade: A (Official iRO Website)
