1. Total Pet Count:

   - data/db/pets.toml lists 25 pets.
   - The 25th pet in mod0631 is BON_GUN (Mob ID 1188, egg 9025 Heretic Egg / Bongun Egg, accessory 10020 Sword of Bongun / Chinese Exorcism Sword, food 537).
   - In the iRO Cute Pet Items table (Category=7), item 10020 ("A ceremonial sword used by chinese exorcists, to lead the dead beyond the realm") is already present in the item database, showing Bongun's assets were present in the client, but Bongun was unreleased as a catchable pet in early 2004 official servers.
