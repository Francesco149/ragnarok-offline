# Task 7: Official Server Rates, Kafra Fares, and Novice Start Cells

## Answer so far
euRO Closed Beta (2003-12-20 to 2004-01-31) operated with boosted EXP and drop rates officially described by General Manager Imdat Solak ("PerrI") as "higher experience". Contemporary community member Pandaroc explicitly stated on 2004-01-27 that Closed Beta rates were 3x ("nämlich 3fach"), while RnS Site Admin Astartus noted rates were "too high". Authentic forum records confirm extraordinary player leveling: user -Barak- reached Level 64/34 Knight on 2003-12-24 (exactly 4 days after launch), Ryoshin reached Assassin in under 72 hours, and =Takeda= reached Thief 48/35 in under 48 hours. Open Beta (Feb 2004) and commercial launch (April 15, 2004) operated strictly at 1x rates, as Open Beta characters were retained.

Before kRO's 2004-02-24 overhaul, Kafra fares were: Prontera to Izlude 900z, Geffen 1400z, Payon 1700z, Morroc 1800z, Comodo 2000z, Orc Dungeon 1700z. Dungeon Kafras permitted saving. euRO ran the Dec 2003 Comodo client and retained these pre-overhaul fares through Closed/Open Beta until the Juno update (scheduled June 2004).

New characters in Dec 2003 spawned at Novice Training Ground bridgehead (`new_1-1.gat` / `new_zone01.gat` cell `53, 111`). When Gravity added NPC Sprakki, she was placed at `(53, 114)` directly facing `(53, 111)`. While YouTube videos (`GJO1B9ogKXY`, `Xf74xcQkHGA`) show this exact bridgehead spawn, frames reveal they are private emulator servers (EosRO 2017 and "Ragnarok 2004"). No surviving 2003 screenshot captures `/where` text on a brand-new character.

## Contradictions and Open Points
1. **Closed Beta Rate Multiplier (Official vs Community)**:
   - Official BHI-OE announcements (Imdat Solak / PerrI) never stated a numeric multiplier, intentionally describing it only as "testing with higher experience" to manage player expectations prior to the Jan 31 wipe.
   - Contemporary player Pandaroc on Gamona explicitly asserted that rates were 3-fold ("nämlich 3fach"), while German RnS Site Admin Astartus stated that both EXP and Drop rates were excessively high.
   - Forum topics on `forum.euro.bhi-oe.de` from Dec 24–25, 2003 (Topic 67: *"what's the exactly exp/Droprate atm?"*; Topic 80: *"[ Poll ] Exp and Droprate : Higher or high enough :D?"*) confirm that the exact multiplier was kept server-side and debated among players. Player Barak achieving Level 64/34 Knight in 4 days provides concrete empirical proof of heavy acceleration.

2. **Kafra Fare Discrepancy (jAthena vs eAthena 817)**:
   - In eAthena 817 (`npc/other/kafra.txt`), copy-paste bugs resulted in mismatches between displayed menu strings and actual zeny deducted: Prontera displayed 1400z for Geffen but deducted 1800z; Alberta displayed 1800z for Prontera but deducted 2100z; Geffen displayed 2100z for Aldebaran but deducted 1800z.
   - In jAthena (`npc_town_kafra.txt`), all menu strings perfectly match deducted amounts (Prontera->Geffen: 1400z; Alberta->Prontera: 2100z; Geffen->Aldebaran: 2100z).
   - Historical reconciliation: jAthena represents the correct period logic; 817 suffered from amateur copy-paste implementation flaws.

3. **Kafra Overhaul Implementation Date on euRO**:
   - kRO implemented the new standardized fares (600z / 1200z / 1800z) on 2004-02-24 (Notice 1182).
   - euRO was operating on the December 2003 Comodo client for Open Beta (Feb 2004) and Commercial Launch (April 15, 2004). The official update plan scheduled Juno for June 2004. Thus, euRO preserved the legacy pre-2004-02-24 fare table until the summer 2004 Juno patch.
