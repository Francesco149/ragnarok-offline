# Official English NPC dialogue from authentic period sources (<= 2004-06)

## Answer so far
Authentic period English dialogue for Ragnarok Online (euRO Dec 2003 client era, Comodo patch) was preserved across official Gravity iRO website captures (Dec 2003–Jun 2004), the period fansite RODataZone (captured Dec 11, 2003, exactly contemporaneous with the Dec 2003 client), and contemporaneous 2002–2004 GameFAQs walkthroughs and job guides. These sources establish official Gravity localization lines, distinguishing authentic Gravity English (including period typos like "Kingom", official NPC names like Jason and Kyulnari rather than emulator romanizations Gaeho and Roberto, and Geffen-based Blacksmith guild location) from early fan translations (such as jAthena/eAthena mod0631 and script 817).

## Contradictions and open points
- **NPC Name Translations vs Fan Translations:** Emulator scripts (like early jAthena/eAthena) often preserved raw romanizations from Korean scripts (e.g. "Gaeho" for Jason in Payon, "Roberto" for Kyulnari in Morroc, "Getsufenst" for Geschupenshtein, "Brad" vs "Comrade Brad"). Official period iRO English uses Jason, Kyulnari, and Geschupenshtein.
- **Trunk Scoring for Archer Test:** Different guides cite either 25 points or 40 points as max/passing; Weggy's FAQ specifies Willows drop 4 trunk types (Trunks 7 pts, Fine-Grained 5 pts, Solid 3 pts, Barren 1 pt; 25 pts needed to pass), whereas official text mentions a total grade out of 40.
- **Blacksmith Guild Location:** Post-Episode 10 guides place the Blacksmith Guild in Einbroch/Einbech, but for the 2003-12 euRO client (pre-Einbroch), the Blacksmith Guild was situated strictly in southeast Geffen (`geffen_in 110, 169`), with Geschupenshtein in Alberta.
- **iRO Bug Report Board Captures:** The dynamic ASP bugcontent pages (`bugcontent.asp?mNum=2&seqNo=...`) were not comprehensively archived by Wayback crawlers due to ASP session and query string limits, though board listing snapshots from 2004-03-22 and 2004-06-05 confirm exact board navigation, maintenance notices, and active bug titles.
