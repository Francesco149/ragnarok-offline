## How to use it

1. Read `HANDOFF-r3.md` §2 first: findings are JSON rows, one fact each,
   checked by `research/check_rows.py`. That protocol stands, with the
   rules the reviews added (`research/notes/round3-review.md`,
   `round4-review.md`):
   * **A claim may only restate its quote.** If the number, the cell or
     the name is not in the quote, it is not in the claim. (Round 4 kept
     to this: no claim was false.)
   * **A computed result carries the command and its output as the quote**
     (a cell check with `research/gat_cells.py`, a `grep -c`, a `git log`).
   * **`source_date` of a file on disk is the file's own date** (`ls -l
     --time-style=long-iso`), or empty. Round 4 dated every mod0631 row
     2003-06-30 and every 817 row 2004-01-20; neither date is anywhere.
   * **Rows from a file that is not a period source are grade D**: a
     later client's tables (`euRO_quests.txt` is a quest log of the 2010s),
     our own extractions. A GameFAQs guide is dated by the version-history
     line that added the section you quote: put that line in `reasoning`.
   * **Before searching the web for a file, look on `data:`** and in
     `docs/PRESERVATION.md`: round 5's best find (T2) was on our own disk.
   * **Date a FAQ's section, not the FAQ.** Round 7 read Weggy's simple
     2-1 job changes as iRO in May 2004. They are text from before his
     year-long break (about mid-2003), and his own v6.1 says the 2-1
     quests were added since. Version numbers and dates go in
     `reasoning` only as the page gives them (round 7 wrote "v3.3
     (2003-11-14)" for a page that says 3.2 and gives no date).
   * **A row's URL is the address the saved page came from.** Round 14
     gave twelve GameFAQs rows Wayback captures of `db.gamefaqs.com` of
     2004 that do not exist (CDX has none) for pages fetched from
     `www.gamefaqs.com/pc/561051-ragnarok-online/faqs/<id>` as captured in
     2010. Cite that capture, with its own timestamp; date the text by the
     version line in `reasoning`. If you think an older capture exists,
     check CDX and say what it returned.
   * **Date a saved page by the capture Wayback serves.** RODataZone's
     `quests.js` is the capture of 2004-05-02; `20040417` (round 13's file
     name) is a date found nowhere, and round 15 copied it into 19 rows.
     The checker prints the capture it was served: use that.
   * **A later emulator is grade D**, whatever it says of itself (round 15
     graded three rAthena rows B), and `source_date` is the source's date,
     never the day you read it (round 15 dated ten RODataZone rows
     2026-11-14).
   * **817's files have their own dates**: `db/mob_db.txt` is 2004-02-09
     (`unzip -l`), not 2004-01-20 (round 16 used that date again, which
     round 4 was told is found nowhere). A git file with no date in it is
     dated by `git log --diff-filter=A` (round 16 dated an rAthena script
     2005-08-01, a date it does not carry; its history starts 2006-01-29).
     The May-2004 English tables are the 2004-05-06 patch
     (`2004-05-06gdata_yuno_lang_g.gpf`), not the exe's 2004-05-07.
   * **An item or monster id not in the quote comes from mod0631's JName
     column** (`db/item_db.txt`, `db/mob_db.txt`, with the line), never from
     memory: round 16 gave eight Ragu Memo rows a wrong id (Zeny Knife 1227
     for 1238, "Wizard Hat 5045" for 2252).
   * **Check an id against the source's own numbers** (DEF, slots,
     location, weight) before it goes in a claim. Round 17 wrote 5043
     (Opera Ghost Mask, DEF 1, Mid) for a guide's "phantom opera mask DEF
     2 for mid-lower", which is 2281.
   * **A list copied from an item database is the item text**, not a
     witness for who could use the item. Round 17's perma-Novice guide
     listed weapons "in the database but cannot obtain in iRO yet", with
     the client's own effect lines.
   * **Ragu Memo is `ragmemo.s33.xrea.com`.** Round 18 cited
     `www.ragmemo.com`, which Wayback has never seen, and a truncated
     timestamp. Cite the capture CDX lists, all fourteen digits.
   * **A GameFAQs page is the version its header names.** Round 18 again
     cited a `db.gamefaqs.com` capture of 2004 that CDX lacks, and dated a
     page of "Version 1.4, Last Updated 2005-07-23" to 2004-01 although
     v1.4's history line rewrote the very section quoted.
   * **progress.md lists the rows as they are.** Round 16's list was off by
     one to two ids and described rows that do not exist.
   * **817's ground skills keep their bonuses in `pc.c`** (Volcano, Deluge,
     Violent Gale), not only `battle.c`: grep the whole `map/` tree before
     writing "absent".
   * **Weggy's 2-1 job changes are mid-2003 text, whatever version you
     read them in.** Round 19 dated "simply talk to the man inside" (the
     Blacksmith), "pay the guildmaster 666 zeny" (the Assassin) and "talk
     to the Wizard there" to v6.3-v6.5 (2004-05/06), although v6.1's own
     history line says "Gravity also added more in-depth quests to the 2-1
     classes, so i'll hopefully be able to update those soon" (the rule
     "Date a FAQ's section, not the FAQ", written for round 7).
   * **A file's date is the file's, not its release's.** 817's
     `map/battle.c` is 2004-02-18, `npc/jobs/merchant.txt` 2004-02-03,
     `db/skill_require_db.txt` 2004-01-28 (`ls -l --time-style=long-iso`);
     round 19 dated all three 2004-02-19, and mod0631's
     `conf/npc_job_merchant.txt` (2003-08-10) 2003-12-18.
   * **progress.md says only what the rows say.** Round 23's progress.md
     gave a jAthena commit (`525287f32997`, "//1176 by shadow") that does
     not exist, an 817 line with a `dir_x[24]` array 817 lacks, and four
     quotes found on no saved page, while its rows were clean. Write
     progress.md from the rows file, not from memory; it carries no
     source the rows lack.
   * **A GameFAQs header date can be impossible.** Psycho Battousai's
     guide (21546) says "Last Updated 2003-02-25" but its author began in
     "late April/early May" and covers iRO's Comodo patch (October 2003).
     Check the text against the date before using it.
   * **Already read, not wanted again (round 23 was all repeats):**
     Kiruwa's Wizard FAQ 30054, Mistiknoname3k 20164, Edge 29457, Weggy's
     Knight and Novice sections, Ragu Memo's `abnormal.html` of 2003-08,
     2003-12 and 2004-02, Battousai X 27646's disclaimer, the iRO guide's
     Sightrasher table, the Novice wear masks (built from the client),
     the Acolyte's ascetics.
   * **Copy a capture's timestamp from CDX, all fourteen digits.** Round
     26 cited `20100509054944` for GameFAQs 20075 (CDX: `20100509054954`)
     and `20100508064530` for 28554 (CDX: `20100508090803`). Neither
     exists. The checker prints the capture it was served: use that.
   * **A guide titled "Beta 2" is iRO's beta 2.** Giga Bahamut's
     Archer/Hunter FAQ (20075) is headed "Last Updated 2003-08-12", but
     its Hunter skills are of 2002-10-20 and 2003-02-22 by its own update
     history, and its title says "Ragnarok Online Beta 2". Round 26 dated
     six rows 2003-08-12.
   * **Do not quote back what THREADS.md already says.** Round 4's rows
     for dyes and for the skill table repeated lines the game was built
     from. If a task has nothing beyond that, write one `none` row per
     place searched and move on.
2. Take tasks from the top; the order is by how much of the game waits on
   the answer. A round need not take them all. Say in `progress.md` which
   ones you took.
3. Write only inside `research/agy-threads/r<N>/` for round N (round 26 is
   next): `rows/<task id>.jsonl`, `pages/` for saved web pages, `check.txt`
   for your own checker run. Scripts you write there to build the rows are
   yours to keep and are not committed (git ignores `r<N>/*.py`): nothing
   may depend on them, so a computed row quotes its command and output itself.
   No summary report: rows only, plus one line per
   task in `progress.md`: "answered" only if every bullet of the task has a
   row or a `none` row; otherwise "partly", naming the bullets left.
4. Do not edit anything else, and do not commit.
5. Sources that are not ours to use: leaked Gravity server files (Aegis).
   Later emulators and wikis (rAthena, Hercules, iRO Wiki, RateMyServer)
   may show *that* something existed; they are grade D for *when* and *how
   much*, and never the only source for a row graded above D.
   **Owner, 2026-11-14:** where period sources cannot settle a question,
   later sources are wanted as the best approximation: early knowledge can
   be incomplete. So when a task's period search comes back empty, add the
   later answer as grade D rows (say which source and its date) instead of
   stopping at `none`.
